How it works

We unroll. We style. You feast.

Three steps, in this order, every single time. The whole thing is designed so the only work left for you is eating.

1. Unroll

The base sheet goes down

A continuous roll of heavy-duty, unbleached Kraft paper runs the length of your table, then gets our ink stamp live on the day, in front of your guests.

2. Style

We build the spread on the spot

Cheeses, cured meats, figs, honeycomb, olives and dips are layered into a flowing landscape, with eucalyptus and rosemary threaded through it. The paper is the platter.

3. Feast

Then it all disappears

Guests graze straight from the sheet. Afterwards there's not a single board to wash: roll up the paper, drop it in the recycling, done in five minutes.

What lands on the paper

Cheeky name. Serious food.

Every sheet is stacked with produce sourced as close to the Peninsula as we can get it: artisan local cheeses, properly cured meats, fruit that's in season this week, and dips we make ourselves.

The menu above is our signature spread. Swap in a favourite, drop something out, or ask us to lean sweeter, saltier or greener. The layout adapts to the sheet, so nothing looks sparse.

Dietaries welcome. Vegetarian, gluten-free and nut-free sections can be styled as their own clearly marked zone on the sheet, so no one has to ask what's safe.
About us

Born from a simple belief.

The best parties don't happen around stiff, formal plates. They happen when everyone gathers around one table, rolls up their sleeves and digs in together.

We specialise in premium flat-lay grazing. That means no bulky rented props, no heavy wooden boards to wash and absolutely zero fuss for you. We arrive at your venue, unroll our signature heavy-duty brown paper and build a breathtaking, edible work of art directly on the surface.

When the party's over? You literally roll up the paper and toss it straight into the recycling. No cleanup. No dishwashing. Just a full belly, amazing memories and a room full of happy guests.

Holy sheet, right?
